Submission requirements
-----------------------

Allowed length: 4-12 double-column pages, or 8-20 single-column pages,
including all figures. This manuscript is 11 double-column pages including
references, within the stated range.

ACM-Reference-Format bibliography style, rather than IEEEtran.

and not under consideration elsewhere. At least one author must register
and attend to present an accepted paper.
